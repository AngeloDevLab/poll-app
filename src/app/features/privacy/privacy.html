2. Overview

Poll App is a learning project for creating and answering surveys. There are no accounts, no cookies and no tracking. Personal data is only processed to deliver the website and the surveys, and to answer your message if you contact me.

3. Hosting of the website

The website runs on my own server at Hetzner Online GmbH, Industriestr. 25, 91710 Gunzenhausen, Germany. The server is located in Germany. When you open a page, the server processes your IP address and the requested page to deliver it. No access logs are stored.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in a secure, working website).

4. Database and live results (Supabase)

Surveys and votes are stored in a Supabase database in Frankfurt, Germany (region eu-central-1). When you open the website, your browser loads the surveys directly from Supabase and keeps a connection open to receive new votes live. Supabase processes your IP address for this and keeps technical request logs for a short time.

Provider: Supabase Pte. Ltd., 65 Chulia Street #38-02/03, OCBC Centre, Singapore 049513. Supabase uses EU Standard Contractual Clauses as a safeguard for transfers outside the EU. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in providing the surveys and their live results).

5. Creating surveys

When you create a survey, its name, description, category, end date, questions and answers are saved together with the time of creation and shown to all visitors. No name, IP address or other reference to you is saved with the survey.

Please do not enter personal information about yourself or others. Surveys cannot be changed or deleted on the website. If you want a survey to be removed, write to the email address above.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (publishing the survey you created).

6. Voting

When you complete a survey, only the chosen answers and the time of the vote are saved. Votes are anonymous: no IP address, no browser id and no other reference to you is saved with them. The results are shown to all visitors.

7. Local storage in your browser

The app stores one entry in the local storage of your browser:

  • poll-app:completed-polls: the surveys you completed and the answers you picked, so you see your answers on your next visit instead of filling in the survey again

This entry is not a cookie and is only saved once you complete a survey. It is never sent to the server and stays until you clear the site data of your browser. Legal basis: § 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG, as it is necessary for the function you use.

8. Contact by email (Google Gmail)

Emails you send me are delivered to and stored in my Gmail mailbox. Provider: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland. Google may process data on servers in the USA. Google is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the EU Commission recognizes as an adequate level of data protection.

I only use your data to answer your message and delete it once your request is completed and no statutory retention periods apply. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering messages).

9. No cookies, no tracking

This app uses no cookies, no analytics tools and no tracking. Fonts and icons are hosted with the website, there is no connection to Google Fonts or similar services.

10. Storage duration

  • Surveys: without time limit, or until their removal is requested
  • Votes: as long as the survey exists, as they contain no personal data
  • Emails: until your request is completed
  • Local storage: until you clear it
